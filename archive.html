Market

China ships 97% of the world's humanoid robots

Chinese makers shipped more than 97% of the roughly 19,100 humanoid robots delivered worldwide in the first half of 2026, according to research firm Smart Analytics Global cited by Bloomberg — with Shanghai's Agibot leading at 8,400 units and Hangzhou's Unitree at 5,900. Industrial and commercial applications made up more than 70% of shipments, up from roughly 50% a year earlier, according to the firm.

Analysis

Why humanoids aren't winning the factory floor yet

Economics, not technology, is the barrier, argues Plus One Robotics co-founder Shaun Edwards in The Robot Report: humanoids costing several hundred thousand dollars can't yet beat specialized sorters hitting 1,300–3,000 picks per hour, while the best humanoid demos manage about 1,000 in controlled conditions. The math changes when manufacturing scale drives component costs down — an argument Edwards says favors mass producers like Tesla.
